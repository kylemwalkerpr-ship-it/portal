## Reapplication Statement Outline

1. Acknowledge previous refusal neutrally.
2. Explain what has changed or what was unclear before.
3. Link each refusal concern to new evidence.
4. Avoid emotional language or guarantees.
5. Keep the explanation concise and evidence-based.
