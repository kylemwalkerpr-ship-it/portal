### Comparable-market signals

- Immigration intake forms range from low-cost generic forms to approximately $10 fillable immigration-law intake forms and ~$29 broader law-firm packets.
- Canada/immigration guides commonly appear around $9–15; PGWP-specific blueprint listings were around the low-teens equivalent.
- Visitor invitation-letter templates commonly sit around USD 10 equivalent; the YouSafe TRV workbook warrants a small premium only because it adds IMM 5257 preparation, itinerary, host support, history and document review.
- Family/travel-history organizers and immigration record books commonly appear around roughly USD 3–13 depending on page count/depth.
- Broad visa/work-permit bundles range from inexpensive commodity templates through roughly $17 checklist bundles to ~$75 professional workbook systems. The Mega Bundle target is therefore $49.99 only because its release contract is 15 individually named QA-tested fillable PDF products, not an 8 KB source ZIP.
- Proof-of-funds and study-plan products compete with substantial free university/government guidance; the paid value must therefore be organization, fillable workflow and cross-document consistency—not access to facts that are already free.

## Truthful delivery contract

- Product 1: **15 individual fillable PDF preparation workbooks**. Do not describe the sale as five resources or as raw source/Markdown files.
- Products 2–9: **one fillable PDF workbook per product**, containing the guided sections named in the listing. Do not imply five separate Word/PDF files when one workbook contains five sections.
- Every immigration workbook states that it is not an official government form, legal advice, legal representation, or a guarantee of approval and directs the buyer to current official sources.

## Interlinking model

Each product has three layers:

1. **Payhip product + Payhip blog:** transaction/use-intent content. Explains what the workbook does, what the buyer receives, and links to the deeper Apex guide.
2. **Apex product-led editorial:** workflow/how-to intent. Links to the exact Payhip product, the YouSafe Marketplace detail page, relevant adjacent products, and authoritative legal/regulatory articles.
3. **Authority layer:** existing YouSafe/CaseWorks legal or regulatory article plus current government source. This layer explains rules; it must not be cannibalized by product copy.

Cross-sells must be situational rather than indiscriminate. The Mega Bundle never cross-sells constituent products because they are already included.
