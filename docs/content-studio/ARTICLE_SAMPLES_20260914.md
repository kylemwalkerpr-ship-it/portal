# Content Studio article samples (Phase 0 / Phase 6 handoff)

These samples are editorial fixtures for Codex review. They are **not** live Grok generations and **not** claimed ranking or conversion results.

## Blind rubric (score each 1-5)

1. Answers the reader actual decision in the first screen.
2. One connected argument; later sections do new work.
3. Claims are sourced or explicitly omitted.
4. Format matches the problem (no kit FAQ/padding).
5. Marketplace CTA is relevant or honestly absent.

Pass = mean >= 4 and no fabricated fee/date/outcome.
