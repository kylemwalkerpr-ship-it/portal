## Australia scope

YouSafe Australia supports public-facing administrative/document-preparation and planning services including:

- Subclass 500 student-visa document preparation and Genuine Student evidence organization.
- Temporary Graduate (Subclass 485) document organization and post-study planning.
- Australian university/program planning and Confirmation of Enrolment timeline support.
- Dependant/family-document organization.
- Settlement and newcomer setup such as housing, banking, OSHC, transport and tax-file-number task planning.
- Routing to an appropriately licensed Australian professional when individualized immigration/legal advice or representation is required.

Canonical Australia site: https://au.yousafeconsultancy.com
