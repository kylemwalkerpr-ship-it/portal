# FAQ snippets (site marketing)

## How does payment work?
You pay into escrow when you accept an offer. Funds stay there until you approve the delivered work, then they release to the provider. If something goes wrong, you can dispute the order and support mediates.

## Who are the attorneys and consultants?
Every attorney on the panel is bar-verified in their stated jurisdiction; every consultant is credentialed (CICC, OISC, or equivalent). Licences are checked on intake and on renewal cycles.

## What countries do you cover?
United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Country-specific professional rules are enforced at the platform level.

## Is my information secure?
TLS in transit, encrypted document storage at rest, Clerk-secured sign-in with 2FA available. Member areas are no-indexed; data is not sold to marketers.

## How quickly can I get matched?
Most inquiries receive an offer within a few hours. Complex matters (appeals, multi-jurisdiction) may take 24–48 hours. These are typical ranges — not guarantees.

## What if I am not satisfied?
Request revisions before approving. If that is not enough, open a dispute — support reviews history, messages, and deliverables.

## Off-platform contact
Do not share personal phone, email, WhatsApp, payment-app handles, or ask to move the engagement off YouSafe. Keep all communication and payment on-platform.
